How contributing works

1. Send a pitch

Tell us your proposed topic, the reader it will help and why it matters.

2. Editorial review

We review relevance, originality, clarity, accuracy and fit with the hub.

3. Agree the terms

Before work begins, we confirm the format, deadlines, editing process, attribution and any fee or other arrangement in writing.

4. Prepare for publication

Approved work is edited with the contributor and published with an agreed author credit.

Contributor guidelines

  • Submit original work that you have the right to share.
  • Use reliable sources and include links or references for factual claims.
  • Do not submit copied work, fabricated research, misleading claims or undisclosed promotional content.
  • Write clearly for a general audience and explain technical terms.
  • Disclose relevant conflicts of interest, sponsorships or affiliate relationships.
  • Do not send confidential client information or personal data about other people.

Submitting a pitch does not guarantee acceptance, publication, employment or payment. Any paid assignment, contributor fee, revenue share or voluntary arrangement must be discussed and agreed in writing before work starts. We do not promise payment based only on page views or commission.
