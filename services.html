How a project works

1. Send your request

Tell us about your goal, budget and deadline.

2. Scope and quote

We clarify the deliverables, working arrangement and fee.

3. Agree and begin

Work starts after both sides approve the terms.

4. Review and deliver

We share progress, complete agreed revisions and hand over.

For ongoing work, a fixed monthly retainer can be discussed and documented before the engagement begins. No work or payment commitment is created by submitting an enquiry.
